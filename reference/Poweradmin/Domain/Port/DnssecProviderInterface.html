DnssecProviderInterface

interface DnssecProviderInterface implements ZoneRectifierInterface, ZoneSigningInterface, ZoneKeyManagementInterface (View source)

DNSSEC operations for one zone.

Union of the DNSSEC provider roles; consumers should depend on the narrowest role they use.
