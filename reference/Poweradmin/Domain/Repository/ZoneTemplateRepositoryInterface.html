ZoneTemplateRepositoryInterface

interface ZoneTemplateRepositoryInterface (View source)

Persistence for zone templates (zone_templ), their records (zone_templ_records) and the zone -> template links kept in zones.zone_templ_id.

ZoneTemplateAccessPolicy owns the permission checks, ZoneTemplateWriteService and ZoneTemplateRecordService the validation, ZoneTemplatePlaceholders the placeholder expansion; every SQL statement behind those decisions lives here.
