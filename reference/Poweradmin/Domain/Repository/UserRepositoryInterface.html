UserRepositoryInterface

interface UserRepositoryInterface implements UserLookupInterface, AuthUserLookupInterface, UserNotificationRecipientInterface, UserPermissionReadInterface, UserCredentialWriterInterface, UserAdminInterface (View source)

Persistence for user accounts and the zones they own.

Union of the UserRepositoryInterface roles; consumers should depend on the narrowest role they use.
