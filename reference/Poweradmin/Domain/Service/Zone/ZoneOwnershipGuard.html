ZoneOwnershipGuard

class ZoneOwnershipGuard (View source)

The last-owner rule: a zone keeps at least one owner of a kind the ownership mode allows. Every writer removes owners through here, so the check and the delete share one transaction and two concurrent removals cannot both pass.
