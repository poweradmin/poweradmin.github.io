ZoneOwnershipLimit

class ZoneOwnershipLimit (View source)

How many zones a user or group may own, and whether a change would exceed it.

A user counts the zones they own directly, a group the zones granted to it; group grants never count toward the members. The limit binds the owner, whoever acts, and a superuser owner is never limited. Lowering a limit keeps existing zones.
