ZoneChangeRequestService

class ZoneChangeRequestService (View source)

Files zone change requests and applies the reviewer's decision.

Filing validates the rows the way a direct save would and stores the diff without writing to the zone. Approval replays the stored actions through RecordManager as the reviewing user, so RecordManager's own permission checks still apply. Who may file or review is decided by the caller.
