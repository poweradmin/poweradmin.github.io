Poweradmin\Application\Controller\Zone

Classes

AddZoneMasterController

Handles the add-primary-zone form: validates name and kind, applies the template and creates the zone.

AddZoneSlaveController

Handles the add-secondary-zone form: validates the name and primary address, then creates the SLAVE zone.

BulkRegistrationController

Handles the bulk registration form: creates a primary zone from a template for each submitted domain name.

ChangeRequestController

Shows one change request with its before/after actions and takes the reviewer's approve or reject decision, or the requester's cancellation.

DeleteDomainController

Handles the delete-zone confirmation page and deletes the zone on confirmed POST.

DeleteDomainsController

Handles bulk zone deletion from the zone lists: shows the selected zones and deletes them on confirmation.

EditController

Renders the zone edit page with its record table and handles record, comment, template and signing changes.

EditZoneMetadataController

Handles reading and replacing raw PowerDNS domain metadata for a single zone.

ListChangeRequestsController

Lists change requests: reviewers see the zones their approve level covers, everyone else sees the requests they filed.

ListForwardZonesController

Renders the forward zone list with pagination, letter filter and sorting; also handles the API sync action.

ListNetworksController

Lists network -> view mappings (PowerDNS 5.0+) and exposes inline add/edit/remove actions. Admin-only; hidden behind the supportsViews() capability gate, which covers the version, the backend and views=yes.

ListReverseZonesController

Renders the reverse zone list with pagination and sorting.

ListViewsController

Lists PowerDNS views (5.0+) and the zones they contain. Also exposes inline add/remove actions. The whole feature is admin-only and hidden when the connected server cannot serve views, whether through its version, its backend or its views setting (capability gate).

SearchController

Handles the search page: finds zones and records by name or content with paging and sorting.

ZoneCatalogController

Manage which zones a producer zone publishes in its catalog.

ZoneOwnershipController

Handles the zone ownership page: adds and removes user and group owners of a zone.
